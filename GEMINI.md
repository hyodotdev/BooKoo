# GEMINI.md

Gemini surface for BooKoo. There is nothing project-specific here on purpose.

**Read [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md).** It is the single source of truth for
project rules, and [`CONVENTION.md`](./CONVENTION.md) for coding conventions.
`CLAUDE.md` and `.github/copilot-instructions.md` point at the same place.

Skills are canonical in `.agents/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`; the per-agent
pointers under `.claude/`, `.codex/`, `.grok/` and `.cursor/` are generated by
`bash .agents/scripts/sync-skills.sh` — edit the canonical file, never a
generated pointer.
