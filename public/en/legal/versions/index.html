History of public legal documents.

Effective: 2026-11-04

2026-11-04

Added optional active-account statistics by country, off by default with withdrawal, country-code-only IP estimation, Vercel processing and unknown/accuracy limits. The Terms body is unchanged.

2026-10-22

The 1.3.0 native update moves Free banners to Unity LevelPlay/ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, retaining Google UMP only for consent. Provider, cross-border processing, contextual-ad and analytics-exclusion disclosures are updated. Previous versions may retain AdMob until updated. The Terms body is unchanged.

2026-10-14

Subscription verification moves to IAPKit (Hyo Dev), which processes opaque app user identifiers, store receipts/purchase tokens and subscription records. Ledger content and card details are excluded. The RevenueCat SDK and ad-event forwarding are removed in the replacement native build; older installed versions retain their previous SDK until updated.

2026-10-09

Added on-device receipt amount/date recognition and confirmation before saving. Disclosed Android ML Kit technical diagnostics and transmission to Google in the United States. Recognition images and text are not sent to an OCR server. The Terms body is unchanged.

2026-09-01

A 25% anonymous EAS Observe installation sample was disclosed to improve release performance. It processes app, device, operating-system, country, language, startup, rendering, memory, network and message-scrubbed native-crash diagnostics. Account or ledger identifiers, amounts, memos, photos, places, router parameters, custom events, automatic JavaScript errors and free-form exception messages are excluded. United States hosting and possible retention of at least 90 days are also disclosed.

2026-08-29

Free-tier advertising below the main tabs and Pro subscription terms were aligned with the launch design. Google Mobile Ads and UMP processing, non-personalized requests, Android advertising-ID blocking, iOS ATT behavior, consent choices, and RevenueCat ad measurement are disclosed. Financial content and account profiles are excluded from ad requests and events.

2026-08-27

In-app subscriptions were introduced, so RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) was added to section 6 as a subscription-status processor. No ledger content is sent to it. There was no earlier public version, so no one requires retroactive notice.

2026-08-26

The initial Privacy Policy and Terms were prepared. Operator details and the production processing configuration were checked for launch. There is no earlier public version.
