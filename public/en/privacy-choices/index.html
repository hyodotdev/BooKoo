Privacy choices and rights

Submit a request in the app or by email.

Effective: 2026-11-04

In the app

Settings > Privacy and data provides personal export, administrator ledger export, and account deletion. Profile and notification choices are editable in Settings.

By email

Email hyo.dev · hyo@hyo.dev for access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal. Include your account email or handle, but never send a password, OTP, receipt, amount, or government ID.

Verification and response

We verify control of the account and do not disclose another member’s data. Any legal restriction is explained with the available scope.

Optional features

Disable push in the app or OS and disconnect social login in the provider and BooKoo settings. Current-location permission is used only for place selection and can be refused or revoked without losing the rest of the ledger.

Country usage statistics

Country usage statistics in Privacy settings are optional and off by default. Turning the option off immediately removes the stored country code and stops later country lookups. The ledger remains available.
